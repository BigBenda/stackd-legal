Stackd Support

Need help, found a bug, or have an idea? Email stackd.contact@gmail.com and we'll get back to you, usually within a couple of days.

You can also send feedback, with a screenshot, from inside the app under Settings → Send Feedback.

Common questions

  • Deleting your data or account: go to Settings in the app, or email us and we'll take care of it.
  • Subscriptions and refunds: these are handled by Apple. Manage or cancel under your Apple ID's Subscriptions settings, and request refunds at reportaproblem.apple.com.
