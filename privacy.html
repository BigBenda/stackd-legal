Privacy Policy

Last updated October 27, 2026

This policy explains what information the Stackd app collects, why, who it's shared with, and the choices you have. Stackd is operated by Zachary Bender, based in Canada ("we", "us"). If you have questions, contact us at stackd.contact@gmail.com.

The short version: we collect only what's needed to run the app. We don't show ads, don't use tracking or analytics tools, and never sell your information.

1. Information we collect

2. How we collect it

  • From you, when you sign up, enter sessions and hands, request an analysis or send feedback.
  • From Apple or Google, if you choose to sign in with them (see "Account details" above).
  • From Apple, through RevenueCat, when you start a subscription or free trial, so the app knows which plan you have.
  • Automatically, as basic server logs when the app connects to our servers.

We don't buy data, and apart from the sign-in and subscription details above, we don't receive information about you from other companies.

3. How we use it

  • To run the app: signing you in, saving your data, and syncing it between your devices.
  • To analyze hands you submit and show you the results later.
  • To manage your subscription: unlocking Premium, applying free trials and keeping track of your monthly analysis allowance.
  • To support you: replying to feedback and fixing the bugs you report.
  • To keep Stackd secure: preventing abuse, such as excessive analysis requests.

We don't use your data for advertising or marketing, and we don't build profiles about you. If we ever want to use your data for a new purpose, we'll update this policy and ask first where the law requires it.

4. Consent and legal basis

By creating an account and using Stackd, you consent to the collection and use of your information as described here, in line with Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA). For users in the EU and UK, the legal bases are:

  • Contract: most processing is needed to provide the app you signed up for (account, storage, sync, hand analysis).
  • Legitimate interests: server logs and abuse prevention, to keep the service secure.
  • Consent: optional things you choose to do, such as attaching a screenshot to feedback. You can withdraw consent at any time.

6. Where your data is stored

Stackd is run from Canada, but our service providers store and process data in the United States, and may use other countries too. That means your information may be subject to the laws of those countries, including access by their courts or authorities. We use providers that protect data with contractual safeguards, such as Standard Contractual Clauses where required.

7. How long we keep it

  • Account and poker data: while your account exists. When you delete your account, it's removed from our database within 30 days.
  • Data on your device: until you delete it in Settings or remove the app.
  • Server logs: up to 30 days.
  • Feedback: up to 12 months, unless it's still needed to fix an issue.
  • Subscription records: kept by Apple and RevenueCat as needed for billing, tax and accounting rules. Cancel your subscription in your Apple ID settings, since deleting your account doesn't cancel it.

8. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access a copy of your data.
  • Correct inaccurate data. Most of it you can edit directly in the app.
  • Delete your data or your whole account. You can do this in the app's Settings, or by contacting us. If you signed in with Apple, deleting your account also disconnects Stackd from your Apple ID.
  • Export your data in a portable format.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Complain to a data protection authority. In Canada, that's the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

To make a request, email stackd.contact@gmail.com. We'll reply within 30 days and won't treat you differently for using your rights. California residents: we don't sell or share personal information as those terms are defined under the CCPA/CPRA.

9. Cookies and tracking

The Stackd app doesn't use cookies, advertising IDs, analytics tools or cross-app tracking.

10. Security

All data is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections and stored with providers that encrypt it at rest. Access is restricted so each account can only reach its own data. You can also lock the app with Face ID. Your iPhone checks your face or passcode, and Stackd never sees or stores biometric data. No system is perfectly secure, but if a breach affects your data, we'll notify you as the law requires.

11. Age requirement

Stackd is for adults 18 and older. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

12. Changes to this policy

If we make meaningful changes, we'll update the date above and tell you in the app at least 30 days before they take effect.
